Cardiology Department

Comprehensive Cardiology Services

Our expert cardiologists use advanced technology and procedures to deliver world-class diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of cardiovascular diseases.

Catheterization & Angiography

Advanced diagnostic imaging to detect blockages in vessels.

Echocardiography & Stress Test

Heart ultrasound combined with stress diagnostic testing.

Pacemaker Implantation

Regulate heart rhythm using state-of-the-art implants.

Heart Failure Management

Structured, patient-centric chronic cardiac therapies.

CABG (Open Heart Surgery)

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting for arterial blockages.

H.E.P (Heart Evaluation)

Comprehensive health evaluation and screening programs.

IVUS (Intravascular Ultrasound)

High-resolution ultrasound imaging from inside the vessels.

OCT (Arterial Imaging)

Optical Coherence Tomography for 3D cross-sections.

TAVI (Valve Implantation)

Minimally invasive replacement of stenosed aortic valve.
