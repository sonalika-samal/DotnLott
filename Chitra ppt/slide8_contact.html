Common Questions

Do you accept Ayushman Yojana / PMJAY?

Yes, Chitra Heart Hospital is proud to be fully empanelled with PMJAY, extending cashless quality healthcare benefits to eligible sections of society.

What specialized diagnostic cardiac tests do you offer?

We provide state-of-the-art diagnostic facilities including Echocardiography, Stress Testing, Angiography, IVUS (Intravascular Ultrasound), and OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography).

Do you have supportive renal care like Dialysis?

Yes, our comprehensive Nephrology department provides high-quality hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis support for chronic and acute renal patients.
